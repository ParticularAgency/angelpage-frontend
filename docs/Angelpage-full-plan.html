Angelpage · client model

The model, the full feature scope, and the delivery plan

One document to send. It recommends how the business should run, then lists every feature with a scope: Now (in the first agreement, already in the product), Setup (in the product, works after keys or legal text), or Next (a later agreement, not promised in the first one). Country and currency are set for each client. Ages below are planning guides, not something the product collects.

1. The model we recommend

A community shop for a cause. People sell pre-loved and surplus goods. Each listing names a cause. Shoppers pay by card. The cause does not join only to receive a transfer. The cause joins to publish a shop link, campaigns, and stories, and to bring its own people in. The payout is the proof that those people acted.

  1. Fee. On every 100 of item price, the platform keeps 10. That 10 pays for the shop, review, checkout, the hold, and support.
  2. Share. The other 90 is shared by the seller. Default is 0 to the seller and 90 to the cause. The seller may keep part of the 90. The example below keeps 18 and gives 72.
  3. Shipping is charged on top. It is not inside the 10 or the 90.
  4. Hold. Card money waits 14 days, then releases if nobody opened a dispute. Operations can release sooner or keep a disputed order on hold.
  5. Shoppers are not charged a membership. A guest can pay with a name and email.
  6. Optional seller tools (a paid spotlight, a social pack, volume tools) are extra fees. They are never taken out of the cause’s share.
  7. First agreement is everything marked Now and the Setup items you choose to turn on. Everything marked Next is a later phase.

Example on a sale of 100, if the seller keeps 20% of the 90. If the seller keeps nothing, the cause receives 90 and the middle block is 0. The platform block stays 10.

8. What a 20-minute meeting shows

  1. Home on a phone. State the 10, then the seller’s choice on the 90.
  2. One live product with a cause amount.
  3. Guest pay, only if the card test is ready.
  4. The cause storefront. Paste a shop address only if that address has been tried.
  5. One reel only if a real video is published. One event only if the goal is real.
  6. Messages on that product. The hold and payouts only if finance is in the room.

Say. Join to grow a community you can link to. Paste your Shopify, WooCommerce, or shop website and approve what we pull in. You choose the cause. After a fee of 10, you choose how the rest is shared. Shoppers can pay as a guest. The money is held for 14 days.

Do not include in the first agreement. The whole payment is donated. Make an offer. One click from Amazon. eBay with no setup. Every website will import. Phone alerts. Download the app. A cause is here only to receive money.
