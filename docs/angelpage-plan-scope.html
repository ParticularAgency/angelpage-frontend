Scope, extras, and how a cause grows a community

This is the plan to think with, not only a feature list. A cause does not join to wait for a bank transfer. They join to gather people: supporters who list, shoppers who come back, volunteers who post, and links they can put on their own site, in a newsletter, and in a room. The money is the proof that the community did something. It is not the only reason to be here.

On a sale of 100, the platform keeps 10. The seller chooses how the other 90 is shared with the cause. Shipping is separate. Card payments are held for 14 days. Country and currency are set for each client. Optional extras below are examples a client can price. They are not a quote.

Decision this plan asks for. Pilot on the included scope. Treat seller extras, shopper facilities, and the community-growth layer as a priced second step. Do not promise the later column in the first contract.

4. Sellers — included, then extra services

The 10 on each sale pays for the shop, the hold, the label path, and review. Extra services are optional. A seller who only wants to list one coat should not be forced to buy them. The benefit has to be obvious or they will not pay.

Rule for extras. Optional fees sit on top of the sale. They do not come out of the cause share. If a seller pays for a spotlight, that fee is the platform’s. The 90 on the item is still split the way the seller chose.

What we expect a cause to do in a pilot. Paste their shop or list ten items, publish one event or one story, copy the storefront link into a newsletter, and name two staff. Success is not “a payout arrived.” Success is supporters using that link, and at least one of those supporters listing or giving without being asked by the platform team.

9. Suggested phasing for a contract

  1. Pilot, in scope only. One cause with a real storefront, guest pay, review before anything is public, one event or story, messages, and the 14-day hold. Paste-a-shop only for an address already tested.
  2. Community growth. Follow, campaign links, impact page, QR, supporter wall, follower updates. This is the step that makes a cause stay.
  3. Seller extras. Spotlight the seller can buy, AI pack if it is no longer included, social pack, spreadsheet photos.
  4. Phone. Home-screen install and alerts, then a store app for shoppers and sellers.
  5. Connections that need a partner’s permission. eBay login, Amazon after approval, stock that updates itself.

Say in the room. Join to grow a community you can link to. Sales and gifts prove it worked. The first release is the shop, the storefront, campaigns, and the hold. Followers, paid spotlights, phone alerts, and Amazon come after, and they do not take money out of the cause share.
