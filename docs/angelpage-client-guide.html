Angelpage · concept brief

A shop where every sale can support a cause

Use this with any client. It shows the concept, the pages a person walks through, who we expect to use each one, and how a storefront fills from a website they already have. Figures below are the rules in the product today. Country, currency, and delivery area are chosen per client. Nothing here is limited to one market.

On a sale of 100: the platform keeps 10. The seller decides how the remaining 90 is shared with the cause. If the seller keeps none, the cause receives 90. Shipping is charged separately. Card payments stay on hold for 14 days, then release if nobody has opened a dispute.

10 fee 18 seller 72 to the cause

Example only: seller keeps 20% of the 90, so 18, and the cause receives 72. Change the seller share and the last two blocks move. The 10 stays.

3. Filling a storefront from a shop they already run

The public storefront is their shop on this platform. Stock can be typed, uploaded, or pulled in.

What we expect the manager to do: open Product sync, paste the shop address, read what was detected, run Sync, then approve drafts. We expect the first import to be drafts, not a silent publish.

When it is one step: a public Shopify catalogue. Paste, detect, sync. No password.

When it is a short setup, once: a locked Shopify shop, or WooCommerce, needs an API key. WooCommerce often needs a key and a secret. After that, Sync is the repeat action. Auto-sync can be turned on after the first clean import.

When the site is not a known cart: if the pages look like products, they are read in. If they do not, the manager sees a clear failure and can use a key or a spreadsheet instead.

5. Every page — what we expect there

Each block is a real route. Show two or three to a client, not the whole list.

Home, for a visitor

/

Slides about choosing the split, then Sustainable, Flexible giving, and Trusted sellers. Promoted products, category rails, “sellers are donors, buyers are givers,” events, and a close about ordinary purchases supporting a cause.

Expect: a first visit of 15 seconds decides if they scroll. Done when they open a category or start a listing. Watch: one hero sentence still says the whole amount goes to the cause. In the meeting, say the 10 fee out loud.

Home, after login

/ for a signed-in person

The same address becomes the shop: promoted items, women, home, jewellery, electronics, bags, an event with a poll, gifts, latest stories.

Expect: a returning shopper starts here, not on About. Done when a rail has live products. An empty rail means do not use this as the opening.

About

/about

People and businesses can support a cause, including a brand moving excess stock.

Expect: a director reads this before a call. Done when they ask to see the shop, not for a longer essay.

How it works

/how-it-works

A switch between sellers and buyers. Sellers: account, photos, listing. Buyers: categories, filters, account, purchase.

Expect: a careful buyer reads this before paying. Done when the steps match the product. Today this page still describes making an offer and a counter-offer. Checkout is the listed price. Change the page before you send the link to a client.

Contact

/contact

Expect: a manager or a board member who will not use chat writes here. Done when a human replies. Do not demo this as a chatbot.

Privacy and terms

/privacy-policy · /terms-of-service

Expect: almost nobody reads them in a demo. A lawyer will. Privacy already mentions phone alerts. Those alerts are not sent yet. Terms still need a signed-off returns policy before anyone promises a refund button.

Catalogue

/product

Women, Men, Children, Clothing, Shoes, Bags, Accessories, Jewellery, Homeware, Books, Electronics, and filters for price, condition, and size.

Expect: parents use Children and Homeware. Fashion uses Women and Shoes. Older shoppers use Books and Homeware. Done when the first screen has items. They will filter. They will not read a paragraph.

Product

/product/[slug]

Up to 10 photos, optional video, condition, brand, size, colour, and the cause share as a number. Favourite, share, add to cart. Some partner items send the shopper away to pay. Those sales do not use the 14-day hold.

Expect: the cause name is why they trust a stranger’s item. Done when they add to cart or message the seller about collection. Assume a local-delivery listing gets a message. A simple courier listing may not.

Post a product

/product/post-product

Photos, cause, split, then courier, personal delivery, collection, or local only. Save a draft or send for review.

Expect: one item in under 5 minutes. Tangible goods. Delivery only in the countries that client has enabled. Done when the listing sits in review, not on the public shop.

Bulk list

/product/bulk-list

Expect: a brand or a stockroom uploads a sheet of up to 50 rows. Done when they know, before they build the file, that photos are added afterwards. Assume they will be unhappy if they discover that at the end.

Favourites

/favourite

Expect: younger shoppers use it as a wish list. Parents save a size they are unsure about. The seller can be told someone saved the item. Done when the heart on the product and this page show the same item.

Checkout

/checkout

Card payment. The card number is not stored here. A guest enters name and email. Shipping address and the cause lines are on the order.

Expect: guests are the older supporters and the one-time givers. Accounts are the parents who come back. Done when a guest finishes without registering.

Affiliate checkout

/checkout/affiliate

Expect: the shopper leaves to pay on the partner site. Done when the client has been told this is not the 14-day hold. Use this page only if their catalogue is actually a partner feed.

Confirmation

/checkout/confirmation/[orderid]

Expect: they look for the cause amount and a way back. Done when a guest can open the order again with the same email.

Find a cause, profile, storefront

/charity/find-a-charity · /charity/find-a-charity/[id] · /charity/store/[id]

Expect: a seller searches for a cause they already know, and a supporter buys because they trust that name. The storefront link is what the organisation puts in a newsletter. Done when search finds them and the storefront is not empty. Assume a failed search means they will not pick a random cause.

Finish organisation signup

/charity/complete-registrations

Expect: a manager adds the registration number and the public profile. Done when this takes minutes. Assume they abandon if it feels like a grant form.

Organisation account

/charity/account/[id]

Expect: the meeting stays on Product sync and the public link. Staff can be limited to listings, orders, messages, and community, so a volunteer does not see payout details. Done when the manager can tell staff access from owner access.

Stories

/blog · /blog/[slug]

Stories, impact, sustainability, fashion, community, guides, news. An organisation can be allowed to publish.

Expect: older supporters read impact. Younger shoppers only arrive if the home strip links here. Done when a post supports a cause they can also give to. Do not open a demo on the blog.

Events, gift, receipt

/events · /events/[slug] · /events/[slug]/donate · /events/donation/success

Cover, dates, a goal that moves, how funds are split, a poll, an optional live link.

Expect: campaign supporters, about 30–70, give here without listing anything. A younger audience comes only if the live session is worth watching. Done when the receipt is something they can forward.

Reels

/reels

Vertical video: play, sound, heart, share, then the product or the cause.

Expect: about 16–35, on a phone. A volunteer films one item. Done when the reel opens the product. Assume an empty reel page should not be in the demo.

Messages

/messaging/chat · /messaging/chat/[conversationId]

The thread keeps the product photo and price. Typing and read receipts work while people have the site open. The same inbox is inside the account.

Expect: questions about fit, pickup, and local delivery. No payment inside the thread. Done when both people see the new line without refreshing. Not in this version: photo attachments, a staff member joining a dispute, an alert when the phone is locked.

Personal account

/user/account/[id]

One address: analytics, profile, listings, affiliate feed, gallery, social, sold, bought, messages, notifications, earnings, delete.

Expect: a donor lives in Listings and Earnings. A buyer lives in Bought and Messages. Done when the demo opens those, not all twelve. Showing every tab makes a workshop look like a control panel.

Login and register

/auth/login · /auth/register

Email, show or hide password, organisation number when the account is an organisation. Social login only if those buttons are really connected.

Expect: under a minute, and they are in after signup. People over 60 type email. People under 30 tap a social button if it works. Done when a dead social button is removed rather than left on screen.

Verify email and reset password

/auth/verify-email · /auth/request-reset-password · /auth/reset-password

Expect: the message arrives. Done when the email domain is verified with the mail provider. Until then, do not demo “forgot password.”

Admin

/admin/login · /admin · /admin/register

Four seats: full admin, operations (orders, disputes, hold and release), content (listings, reviews, promoted items, imports, gallery, social, stories, events), finance (revenue and payouts). Audit log. Failed payments.

Expect: a board member wants to see who can release money. Done when they see that public admin signup is closed. The register address sends people to login. New admins are created by an existing full admin.

Internal preview

/internal-home

A layout check for the build team. Leave it out of every client link.

8. Phone, as a later expectation

Every age above can use the site in a phone browser now. The layout already adjusts. There is no home-screen install, no phone alert, and no store app yet.

  • Next. Add to home screen, and an alert for a new message, shipped, delivered, and funds released. On an iPhone, alerts of that kind work after the icon is installed, on a recent system.
  • After that. A store app for shoppers and sellers: camera listing, pay, orders, chat, alert when the app is closed. Admin, payouts, and shop keys stay on the website.
  • What we expect clients to ask. Under 35 ask for the app in the first month. Over 60 do not, if guest pay and the receipt email work.

9. A 20-minute showing, for any client

  1. Home on a phone. Point at the split. Say the 10 fee out loud.
  2. One live product with a cause amount. Guest pay only if card testing is ready.
  3. Their kind of storefront. If they have a Shopify or Woo shop, paste it and show Detect. Run Sync only if you have already tried that address.
  4. One reel, only with a real video. One event, only with a real goal.
  5. Messages on that product. Hold and payout only if finance is in the room.

Leave closed for every client: forgot-password until mail works, social auto-post until tokens work, Amazon, make-an-offer, and “download the app.”

Say: Paste your shop. We pull the products in for approval. You choose the cause. After a 10 fee, you choose how the rest is shared. Shoppers can pay as a guest. The money is held for 14 days.
